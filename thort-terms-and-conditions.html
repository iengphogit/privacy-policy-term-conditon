Capture and storage limits

Available cameras, formats and simultaneous capture depend on your iPhone. Heat, low storage, calls, interruptions or other device conditions can stop a recording or export. Storage safeguards reduce risk but cannot guarantee that every recording will finish. Check framing and sound and keep separate copies of important videos.

Deletion and copies

Confirmed deletion from the app is permanent. Copies in Photos or another service are managed separately. Removing the app can remove local recordings and drafts. Export anything you want to keep first.

External cameras and other services

Connect to a camera or stream only when you own it or have permission from its operator to access and record it. A reachable address or publicly visible camera webpage alone does not establish that permission. Respect the operator's conditions and the rights of people shown. You are responsible for the stream addresses and credentials you provide. Thort does not supply third-party footage or promise that a particular camera service will remain available.

Current IP-camera scope

This version receives HTTP(S) MJPEG and HLS video. Direct RTSP, USB cameras, ONVIF discovery, remote lens controls and PTZ are not supported. An RTSP source needs a separately configured relay. IP-stream sound is not recorded; audio comes from the selected iPhone microphone. Network delay and buffering mean different sources are not synchronized at exposure time. Network loss may stop a take; reconnection does not automatically restart recording.
