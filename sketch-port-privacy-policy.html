Privacy policy

Updated 15 October 2026 · SketchPORT MVP1 (0.18.0-rc3)

This notice covers the offline MVP1 release. Operator: PHO ieng, an individual developer in Cambodia. Support and privacy contact: iengpho@gmail.com.

1. Offline app and local data

SketchPORT MVP1 is free and ad-free. It requires no account, login, access key, subscription or payment. AI chat, connections, activation and account services are unavailable. The app has no Android internet permission and does not send projects, messages or device information to the operator or an AI service. It contains no advertising or analytics SDK and does not upload crash reports.

Projects, source files, saved versions, activity history, settings and the educational-safety acknowledgement stay on your device. Compilation uses the bundled local toolchain. USB device details and serial output are processed locally to communicate with a board. Serial Console keeps a limited receive buffer in memory and disconnects when you leave its screen; it does not upload output. Android app backup is disabled.

2. Your actions and permissions

Android requests permission before communicating with a USB board. Firmware uploads and serial commands affect connected hardware. Keep the Serial Console screen open while receiving output. MVP1 has no background capture service and does not request notification permission.

Import and export use files you select through Android. If you choose a cloud-backed document provider or share an export through another app, that service handles the copy under its own terms. SketchPORT does not automatically upload your workspace. Export only information you are authorized to share.

3. Retention and deletion

Local projects and versions remain until deleted or app storage is cleared. Moving a project to Trash does not permanently erase it. Export work you want to keep before using Android App info → Storage → Clear storage or uninstalling. Delete exported files and copies in other apps separately.

If you installed an earlier test build, old AI chat history or encrypted credentials may remain in private app storage. MVP1 cannot use them or send new AI requests. Clearing app storage removes those local records along with your projects. This does not revoke an external service key or erase information previously sent from an older build; contact that service separately.

4. Support and external links

If you choose to email support, PHO ieng receives your email address, message and attachments in Gmail. These are used to answer your request and retained while needed for that purpose or applicable obligations. Request deletion at iengpho@gmail.com. Do not send passwords, access keys or unnecessary personal information.

Policy and developer links open in a separate browser. The host can receive connection metadata under its own privacy policy. Hosted policy pages use GitHub Pages: https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement . Bundled offline documents make no network request. Exported policy pages contain no advertising or analytics scripts and use local fonts.

5. Audience and changes

SketchPORT is intended for learners aged 13 and older, teachers and adults, not children under 13. Users under 18 should have guardian permission and suitable adult supervision for hardware experiments. Contact the privacy address if you believe a child under 13 submitted personal information through support.

Future online or paid features are not included in this release. Material changes to processing will be disclosed before those features are offered.
