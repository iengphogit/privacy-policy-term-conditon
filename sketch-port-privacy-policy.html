Privacy Policy

Updated 8 October 2026 · SketchPORT 0.18

This notice describes SketchPORT 0.18, including optional direct DeepSeek assistance. Operator: PHO ieng, an individual developer in Cambodia. Support and privacy contact: iengpho@gmail.com.

1. Local workspace

Projects, saved versions, settings, trash and chat history are stored in the app’s private device storage. Workspace exports contain selected project data; saved provider keys are excluded. Shared or exported copies are governed by the destination you choose.

The app does not include advertising or analytics SDKs and does not request location, contacts, microphone or camera access. The KM/EN language choice is stored locally. Nokora fonts are bundled; no network font request is made.

2. Optional direct DeepSeek requests

When you agree to AI sharing and send a message, DeepSeek receives your conversation and the selected scope’s source files, project names, IDs and board information. Workspace scope includes all projects, including Trash. Project scope limits the workspace snapshot to that project. Earlier messages may contain previously shared context.

Requests go directly to https://api.deepseek.com over HTTPS using your own DeepSeek API key. SketchPORT does not route these requests through ReanAI or an operator-owned AI gateway. The receiving provider can read the submitted content and applies its own terms, retention and processing policies. Processing may occur outside your country. This notice does not promise zero provider retention or exclusion from training.

Agent steps and history compaction can send additional requests and incur provider API charges. If enabled, serial-monitor capture can become part of chat context. Review shared information before sending. Do not submit secrets, confidential material or personal records without authorization.

DeepSeek terms: https://cdn.deepseek.com/policies/en-US/deepseek-terms-of-use.html
DeepSeek privacy policy: https://cdn.deepseek.com/policies/en-US/deepseek-privacy-policy.html

3. API keys and configuration

On Android 6 and newer, keys are encrypted in private app storage using Android Keystore protection. A Keystore alias identifies protected key material; it is not your provider API key. Hardware-backed protection varies by device. Saved keys are input-only and are never displayed in settings or included in workspace exports. The provider settings window blocks screenshots and recent-app previews.

Add provider opens a secure key-entry dialog. Saving the provider stores its key locally and adds a connection to the provider list. The provider and model chooser is the top control in the chat sheet. Test connection and Fetch available models each send an authenticated request to DeepSeek’s model endpoint; no workspace data is included. Opening settings does not automatically make a provider request. Chat sends the selected model and conversation context to DeepSeek.

Upgrading from the legacy gateway removes the saved ReanAI key. It is never converted into, or transmitted as, a DeepSeek key. Legacy profile metadata is retained locally as an opaque migration backup.

4. Consent and permissions

The first chat-sharing disclosure names DeepSeek. Declining keeps the draft and prevents that action. AI Providers → Withdraw AI-sharing consent stops an active AI task and requires agreement before future chat requests. Previously sent data cannot be recalled. Removing a saved key stops the local AI task and removes the local credential; it does not revoke your provider key or delete provider records. Offline tools remain available.

Android requests access before communicating with a supported USB board. Uploading or serial commands affect attached hardware. Notifications identify active AI or serial tasks. The educational notice acknowledgement is stored locally and is separate from AI-sharing consent.

5. Retention and deletion

Projects, versions and chats remain until you remove them or clear app storage. Moving a project to Trash, starting a new chat or compacting history does not necessarily delete earlier data. To erase private app data, use Android App info → Storage → Clear storage, or uninstall, after exporting work you wish to retain. Delete exported copies separately.

Contact DeepSeek about its processing and provider-side records. Contact PHO ieng at iengpho@gmail.com for SketchPORT privacy questions. Never include a full API key in support messages. Removing the app cannot erase information already sent to another party.

6. Optional app and device verification

Settings → App & device security checks the installed signing certificate and common root/custom-system indicators locally. These checks do not prove a device is secure. When online verification is configured and you choose Check with Google Play, a disclosure appears before a request is sent. Google Play processes app, account/licensing and device integrity information under its own policies. The configured verification service receives an integrity token, app package and random request ID; it receives no source code, chat content or provider API key. Internet connection metadata can be visible to Google and the service host. Offline learning remains available if checks fail.

Online verification is not enabled in this release. No integrity or purchase token is sent by the app in this configuration. Before enabling verification, we will identify the verification service and disclose its processing and retention. Do not include purchase or integrity tokens in support messages.

7. Plans and updates

Optional AI uses your own DeepSeek key. This release has no paid provider unlock, API-credit wallet, top-ups or subscriptions. Provider API charges remain separate. Any future paid feature or new processing will be described before it is offered.

SketchPORT is intended for learners aged 13 and older, teachers and adult users; it is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has submitted personal information through SketchPORT, contact the privacy address above; local app data and provider-side data require the separate deletion steps described in section 5. The app does not verify age. Children under 13 should not use this app. Users under 18 should have parent or guardian permission and appropriate adult supervision for hardware experiments. DeepSeek requires users under 18, or the applicable local age, to use its services only with legal-guardian consent. Provider eligibility requirements still apply to your own account. Educational use requires appropriate supervision. Material changes to processing must be disclosed before they take effect.

8. Support and public policy pages

If you choose to email support, PHO ieng receives the email address, message and attachments you send. The support mailbox is hosted by Gmail. These records are used to answer and resolve your request and retained while needed for that purpose or applicable legal obligations. You may request deletion at iengpho@gmail.com; this does not delete records held by DeepSeek or copies you exported. Do not send API keys, passwords or unnecessary personal information.

Opening an online policy page makes a separate request to GitHub Pages through your browser. The host can receive normal connection metadata under its own privacy policy: https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement . The offline policy pages make no network request; the hosted pages use local fonts and contain no advertising or analytics scripts.
