AirZcreen Desktop Privacy Policy

Updated: October 26, 2026 · Version 0.1.0

Developer and contact

AirZcreen Desktop is a free macOS application developed by PHO IENG, an individual developer in Cambodia. This policy describes version 0.1.0. For privacy questions or support, contact iengpho@gmail.com.

No developer data collection through the app

AirZcreen Desktop does not automatically send personal information, screen content, usage analytics, or diagnostic reports to the developer. It has no user accounts, advertising, purchases, subscriptions, marketing messages, SMS features, or cloud relay. The developer does not sell your personal information or use the app to track you across apps or websites.

What the app processes

AirZcreen captures the independent virtual display it creates. Anything you move to that display can be visible to the paired browser, including personal information shown in other applications. The app does not use the physical display as a fallback capture source.

Video frames, local network addresses, connection details, a pairing PIN, session credentials, and stream statistics are processed on your devices to connect them and display the stream. Screen Recording permission is managed by macOS. The app captures video without audio and does not request access to your camera, microphone, contacts, photos library, SMS messages, or precise location. The browser receiver is view-only and does not provide remote keyboard or mouse control.

Where information goes

The video travels directly between your Mac and one paired browser using WebRTC, intended for a trusted private local network. No external STUN server, TURN relay, or developer server is configured. Media transport is encrypted. The receiver page and pairing/signaling requests use HTTP, which is not encrypted. Someone able to observe or alter that network traffic could obtain pairing or session information or interfere with the connection; encrypted media alone does not remove this risk.

Use a trusted private network and a device you trust. Do not expose the service through router port forwarding, a public tunnel, or an untrusted network. The app is not a network firewall; VPNs and network configuration may affect who can reach it. Anyone who can reach the service and obtains the current PIN may attempt to pair. Keep the PIN private. The QR code contains the local receiver address, not the PIN.

The developer does not receive your screen content, PIN, or network traffic through an AirZcreen server. Your operating system, browser, and network services have their own data practices, outside this app’s control.

Storage and retention

The app does not save recordings or screenshots of the stream. Captured frames, the local preview, and active pairing credentials are held in memory for operation and released when the virtual-display session stops. Disconnecting revokes the viewer's credentials and rotates the PIN, but the virtual display and local capture remain active until you stop them. Stopping the virtual display closes the service and stream and clears the app's frame and preview references.

Your watermark preference is saved locally in macOS app preferences and remains until reset or removed. Your operating system and browser may retain their own history, caches, window state, or diagnostic logs. The app does not automatically upload those records to the developer. Explicit developer test modes can print local diagnostic information, including a temporary loopback pairing code; these test logs are not uploaded automatically.

The receiver keeps its session token in memory rather than a URL, cookie, or local-storage entry. It does not include analytics, advertising cookies, or third-party scripts. A paired device can still take screenshots, record its screen, or retain information independently; the app cannot prevent that.

Your choices

You can disconnect a browser or stop the virtual display at any time. macOS also lets you revoke Screen & System Audio Recording permission. Choose carefully what you move to the extra desktop and whom you permit to view it. There is no AirZcreen account or cloud screen-history database to delete.

Support and external links

If you contact the developer, the developer receives the contact details and message or attachments you voluntarily send and uses them to respond to your request. Support correspondence is kept only as needed to handle the request, maintain relevant support records, or meet applicable obligations. You can request access, correction, or deletion of that correspondence at iengpho@gmail.com; applicable legal requirements may limit a request. There is no fixed 24-month retention period for screen streams, and no developer backup of your streamed screen.

Do not send passwords, pairing codes, or private screen content. Email and the Facebook link open external services only when selected; those services process information under their own policies. AirZcreen does not embed Facebook tracking or load Facebook content in the viewer.

Public policy and support website

The public policy and support pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. They contain no advertising, analytics scripts, forms, or tracking cookies added by the developer. GitHub processes visitor information separately as the hosting provider, including logging visitor IP addresses for security. See GitHub's General Privacy Statement: GitHub General Privacy Statement. Reading this public website is separate from streaming between your devices; the app bundles its policy for offline access and does not send your stream to GitHub.

Children

The app does not request ages, create child profiles, or knowingly collect children's personal information through the app. Children should use screen sharing with a parent or guardian's guidance and avoid displaying personal information. Contact the developer about any child's information voluntarily sent in a support message.

Policy changes

Material changes to collection, storage, or network services require an updated policy. The updated date above identifies this policy version. Statutory privacy rights that apply to you are not limited by this policy.
