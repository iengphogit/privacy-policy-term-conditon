3. Hardware, code and AI

Review code before running it or uploading firmware. Keep copies of your work and independently check wiring, ratings, calculations and results. Uploading replaces a board's program and may actuate connected equipment. AI output may be inaccurate, incomplete or unsuitable; it is not professional advice.

Use a clean, dry, well-lit and suitably ventilated workspace with protective equipment appropriate to the experiment and laboratory procedures. Do not use SketchPORT to guide mains AC, high-voltage, high-current or exposed-live-wiring experiments. Never connect mains electricity directly to Arduino, a phone, USB or a breadboard. Low voltage alone does not guarantee safety.

Uploading, resetting, opening a serial connection, reconnecting USB or restoring power may restart firmware or change output states. Connected motors, relays, heaters and other loads may activate unexpectedly. Failed uploads, lost connections and app errors do not establish that equipment is safe. Keep people clear, disconnect hazardous loads before testing, and use a controlled setup with qualified supervision. Follow the Educational use & safety notice and equipment manufacturer's procedures. Do not leave experiments unattended or use SketchPORT as a protective device or emergency-stop system.

Cancelling AI, closing SketchPORT or unplugging the phone does not stop firmware already running on a powered board, reverse an upload or necessarily switch off outputs. Externally powered boards may continue operating. Use appropriate physical power isolation or equipment-specific stop controls when safe; do not rely on app cancellation for hardware safety.

If operation is unexpected, stop the experiment, keep others away and obtain qualified help before resuming. Follow local emergency procedures for injury, fire or immediate danger. App support is not an emergency service. Report suspected faults to iengpho@gmail.com without API keys, passwords or confidential information you are not authorized to disclose. These precautions explain responsibilities and do not waive rights or liabilities that cannot lawfully be excluded.

AI may propose workspace changes. Stopping an agent does not undo changes already applied. Saved versions and exported backups can help recover work but are not a guarantee against data loss.
