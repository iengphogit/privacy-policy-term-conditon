AirZcreen Desktop User Disclaimer

Updated: October 26, 2026 · Version 0.1.0

Before you share

AirZcreen Desktop creates an independent virtual display and sends its video to a paired browser. Anything visible on that display can be seen by the receiving device, including personal information shown in other apps. Share only content you are authorized to show and obtain permission when another person's information is involved. Keep passwords, private messages, financial details, and other sensitive content off the shared display.

Use trusted devices and a private network

Use AirZcreen only on a trusted private network with a receiving device you trust. WebRTC media is encrypted, but the receiver page, pairing PIN exchange, and connection setup use unencrypted HTTP. Someone able to observe or alter that traffic could obtain pairing or session information or interfere with the connection. Do not expose the receiver through public Wi-Fi, router port forwarding, or a public tunnel. Keep the pairing PIN private.

The recipient can keep a copy

A receiving device can take screenshots, record its screen, or share what it sees. AirZcreen cannot prevent this or erase copies made on another device. Disconnecting or stopping ends access to future frames; it does not recall information already viewed or saved.

Save your work and know how to stop

Save important work before starting or stopping a virtual display. Disconnect revokes the current browser and changes the PIN, but local display capture continues until you select Stop Virtual Display. The emergency shortcut is Control + Option + Command + Escape. Stopping removes the owned virtual display; macOS manages where its windows return. Recovery controls cannot guarantee recovery from every operating-system or hardware failure.

Availability and limitations

The current version is development software. Display creation, connection quality, frame rate, and compatibility can vary with macOS, hardware, browser, and network conditions. There is no promise of uninterrupted operation or suitability for every purpose. Do not rely on AirZcreen for emergency response or other safety-critical decisions. A disclaimer does not fix the app's unencrypted HTTP connection setup or other technical limitations.
